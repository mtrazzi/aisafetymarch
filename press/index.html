Susan Rice Calls “But China” Argument “BS” as Marchers in Cities Demand an AI Treaty Ahead of Trump-Xi Meeting

San Francisco and Washington, DC · November 17, 2026

On Saturday, November 14, over 1,000 protesters marched in cities across countries, calling on President Trump and President Xi to begin negotiating an international treaty that halts the development of more powerful AI systems until it can be done safely. The marches took place days before the two leaders are due to meet.

In Washington, DC, which held its first-ever AI safety march, protesters marched from the US Capitol, where Ambassador Susan Rice, former National Security Advisor and UN Ambassador, and Nate Soares, president of the Machine Intelligence Research Institute, addressed the crowd. In San Francisco, protesters rallied outside OpenAI and marched to the Veterans Building, where the UN Charter was signed in 1945. Former OpenAI researcher Daniel Kokotajlo spoke at a protest outside his former employer for the first time.

In New York, protesters marched from Trump Tower to the Chinese mission to the UN, with signs in Chinese and English reading “Stop the China-US AI race!” In Boston, the College Democrats of Massachusetts and the Massachusetts AI Action Network (MAIAN) organized a protest in partnership with the march. Philadelphia and Chicago also marched.

Beyond the US, Matthew Tromp spoke in Montréal for Stop the AI Race, as part of Canada’s new Coalition for an AI Treaty, which includes PauseAI Canada. Vancouver also marched. In Paris, Berlin and Hamburg, Pause IA, PauseAI Germany, Stop the AI Race and Stop IA France marched together, with partner events as far away as Australia.

The March for an AI Treaty was organized by Stop the AI Race with . It follows marches in March, July and September, and a 39-day daily presence outside OpenAI, asking Sam Altman to urge the US government to negotiate an AI treaty.
